# Research licensing and distribution boundaries

Original Research code and documentation by ZhongTyler use the bundled MIT license. Third-party archives retain their upstream licenses, copyrights and notices; see [MCP notices](integrations/mcp/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt) and its source/package locks. This grant does not relicense referenced articles, videos, screenshots, services or user-collected research.

REA (`morluto/rea`, `rea-agents@6.4.0`) and the matching official skill references retain their [MIT license](integrations/rea/vendor/REA-LICENSE.txt). Its pinned archive and integrity are recorded in [source-lock.json](integrations/rea/source-lock.json); transitive dependencies retain their own licenses and are not bundled as an installed runtime.

The package includes pinned source and top-level npm archives, not a complete offline dependency mirror. Browser extensions and media models/runtime dependencies are optional downloads and retain their own terms. Unlicensed Stack Overflow source archives, private research, credentials, browser profiles and host scheduler state are excluded. Service access and redistribution of collected content require the recipient's own authority.
